# BLS oracle corpus provenance

Every output, verdict and decode outcome in these files is what Dash Core's own
BLS code returned through the consensus oracle's `oracle_bls_exec` (ABI 14). The
inputs are generated by `contrib/vectors/bls_oracle` (see its README.md).

## Oracle

- Dash Core commit `0dd12af0644b5b3aafc8d60cb2a786292d7788b0` (branch `node/bs12-oracle-bls-v24.0`, the program's v24.0
  pin): v24.0.0-rc.3 plus the test-only consensus oracle shim. `src/bls/` and `src/dashbls/` are unchanged from
  v24.0.0-rc.3.
- Archive `libdash_oracle_v24_0.a` sha256 `3af88a4b55009c08797ab79952f1c18d39264e94eeccde1607ca1ca9ac5ee18d`, built
  reproducibly by rust-dashcore's `contrib/node/build-oracle.sh` (plain flavour; `check-oracle-repro.sh` found it
  byte-identical in two cache roots).
- Probe: `contrib/vectors/bls_oracle/build.sh <archive> <probe>`.

## hash_to_g2.json5

- Content: the scheme's hash to G2 (HASH_TO_G2, i.e. `CBLSSecretKey(1).Sign(h)`) of seeded messages, both schemes.
- Generator: `gen.py --probe <probe> --out-dir pkgs/pkc/corpus/bls_oracle`.
- `hash_to_g2.json5` sha256 `feea4521f81fb3c79f9c1dc463166cc6f9cff548e3b839cdf23af551374887a2`

## sign.json5

- Content: keys (PUBKEY), signatures (SIGN) and VerifyInsecure verdicts for the signed message, another message, another
  key, a negated signature or key, and a zero (invalid) key or signature, both schemes.
- Generator: `gen.py --probe <probe> --out-dir pkgs/pkc/corpus/bls_oracle`.
- `sign.json5` sha256 `404dd2caa1cf17fb0cb6aeed88cb31e2421de757c47c8aa302dc334aa980d4e8`

## aggregate.json5

- Content: AggregateInsecure of secret keys, keys and signatures, SubInsecure, and VerifyInsecureAggregated over
  same-message and distinct-message sets, both schemes.
- Generator: `gen.py --probe <probe> --out-dir pkgs/pkc/corpus/bls_oracle`.
- `aggregate.json5` sha256 `0597d377c2a1ef11b2c6b8bb093ab93243e531497bfbf0d0717959c67e2e625b`

## secure.json5

- Content: AggregateSecure of 1 to 7 signers (a signer twice, reversed order) and VerifySecureAggregated against
  reordered or missing keys, another message and an insecure aggregate, both schemes.
- Generator: `gen.py --probe <probe> --out-dir pkgs/pkc/corpus/bls_oracle`.
- `secure.json5` sha256 `e690d593b72ddeaeee6bda1268757ce3c4ce31a482fd70a17dbc6334b18592bc`

## threshold.json5

- Content: SecretKeyShare, PublicKeyShare and Recover for thresholds 1 to 4 over full, short and duplicated id subsets,
  including the cases Core refuses, both schemes.
- Generator: `gen.py --probe <probe> --out-dir pkgs/pkc/corpus/bls_oracle`.
- `threshold.json5` sha256 `2abe1c0e2a932599473c41e92062bb750e21351b96c8a9e2c03ba385203040a0`

## ies.json5

- Content: CBLSIESMultiRecipientBlobs encryption with a fixed ephemeral key and IV seed, Decrypt of each blob, and the
  CBLSIESEncryptedBlob single-recipient form, both schemes.
- Generator: `gen.py --probe <probe> --out-dir pkgs/pkc/corpus/bls_oracle`.
- `ies.json5` sha256 `aa6952cfbec6c26c68da07b76c06f59c07d18ff286746e93d0636584c2d79dbd`

## decode.json5

- Content: CBLSWrapper (eager) and CBLSLazyWrapper (lazy) decoding of bls_wrapper's input classes under each scheme
  flag: VALID, INVALID or a throw, and the re-serialization.
- Generator: `gen.py --probe <probe> --out-dir pkgs/pkc/corpus/bls_oracle`.
- `decode.json5` sha256 `2e113172741a267bcad767857eedb6feb2d6a137eeca69255e6478045f28d1dc`

## edge.json5

- Content: VerifyInsecure, VerifyInsecureAggregated and VerifySecureAggregated over invalid (zero) operands (including
  an AggregateSecure made with an invalid key) and, decoded under the legacy scheme, keys and signatures outside the
  prime-order subgroup or off the curve (bls_wrapper's tors_, nonsub_ and off_ classes), each decoded under one scheme
  and verified under either flag.
- Generator: `gen.py --probe <probe> --out-dir pkgs/pkc/corpus/bls_oracle`.
- `edge.json5` sha256 `0cab52634d69b4f425a6695b848816b2849e1e30a939478b86030d7934304ae5`

## dkg.json5

- Content: a regtest DKG transcript; see below.
- Generator: `dkg.py --probe <probe> --run <run dir> --argv <argv log> --out dkg.json5`.
- Run: Core's `test/functional/feature_llmq_dkgerrors.py` from the v24.0.0-rc.3 tree (0364ed626e), PRNG seed
  5249215831811370464, against the release `dashd` 24.0.0-rc.3 (sha256
  `e204e3163185b1bffcd3bf3532c0957d8cc11019eaf8f3dbc3b2fc82a7ab5bb4`) run with `-capturemessages`. Core's members are
  random, so a new run gives a new transcript.
- Kept: the type-100 sessions' qcontrib, qcomplaint, qjustify, qpcommit, qfcommit and qsigrec messages, the members in
  Core's order (debug.log), the operators' regtest keys (the logged command lines), and every share, aggregate and
  verdict Core derives.
- `dkg.json5` sha256 `e5d2163fefe7090b7c5357d57ffd98396053b1459e0b42bf038e2f7731bdcaa2`
